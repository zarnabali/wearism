# Wearism - AI-Powered Personal Stylist & Virtual Wardrobe

## Mission
Wearism's mission is to revolutionize fashion by helping users rediscover their own clothes, reducing textile waste, and simplifying daily styling through advanced artificial intelligence.

## Core Features
1. AI Digitization: Automatically tag and organize your closet from photos.
2. AI Outfit Generation: Get personalized daily outfit recommendations based on your style and the weather.
3. Smart Swap: A sustainable peer-to-peer logistics system for swapping clothes you no longer wear.
4. Privacy-First Matching: We protect user data while connecting them with sustainable fashion vendors.

## Technology Stack
- Next.js (App Router)
- React
- Tailwind CSS
- GSAP & Framer Motion for animations
- AI-driven image recognition and stylistic matching

## Sustainability Commitment
Wearism is dedicated to the circular economy. By unlocking the hidden potential of existing wardrobes (the "Closet Paradox"), we help users shop less and wear more.

## Target Audience
- Fashion enthusiasts looking for styling inspiration.
- Sustainability-conscious consumers.
- Busy professionals wanting to reduce decision fatigue.
